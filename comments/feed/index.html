ABOUT
Remote Lakefront Villa on Lago de Atitlan for Sale: USD 549,000

On the quiet shores of Lake Atitlán stands a rare lakeside property designed by renowned Atitlán architect John Pennington. A house with atmosphere, history, and an almost forgotten beauty. Far from the noise and constant movement of lakeside villages, the property lies in one of the most beautiful and peaceful bays of the lake, where the days still start slowly and the landscape still feels wild and untouched.

The Bay

Located near natural volcanic hot springs, the property enjoys one of the most peaceful and secluded settings around Lake Atitlán. Hidden between Tzununa and Jaibalito, the house is one of only three properties along this quiet stretch of shoreline, where nature, silence, and the rhythm of the lake still define daily life.

Designed by
John Pennington

Built in the spirit of old Atitlán, the architecture reflects a more personal and artistic era of lakeside design, standing in quiet contrast to many of today’s more modern and minimalist styles. Rather than emphasizing sharp lines and expansive glass surfaces, the house offers warmth, texture, handcrafted details, natural materials, and a deep connection to the surrounding landscape.

The Vision

This is a place for visionaries, creators, and doers. Not a finished luxury product, but a rare and deeply atmospheric property waiting for the right person to bring it back to life.

Bring your own ideas. Invest your energy, creativity, and passion into it, and create something far more meaningful than modern perfection. Many of the most extraordinary places begin exactly like this.

WILD STEEP GARDEN, ENDLESS LAKE
An Old Lakeside Estate

The villa consists of two separate apartments surrounded by an old steep lakeside garden that has slowly been reclaimed by nature. Stone paths disappear beneath greenery, mature plants lean toward the water, and every corner feels as if it belongs to another time. It is not a polished luxury property. It is something far more unusual: a place with soul.

LAKE PHILOSOPHY 
Silence, Water, Volcanoes, Freedom.

From the terraces and living spaces, the lake stretches out wide and quiet, framed by the volcanoes of Atitlán. In the early mornings, the water lies smooth and still beneath a veil of mist. By midday, the Xocomil wind arrives, transforming the lake into a wilder, more dramatic landscape of moving waves and shifting skies. The private dock sits directly at the shore, inviting long swims, slow afternoons, or evenings spent watching the changing mood of the water.
